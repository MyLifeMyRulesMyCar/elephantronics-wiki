- **Ready to build.** Go straight to the [DHT22 tutorial](/microcontrollers/dht22-arduino-uno-oled/), then use the [circuit design checklist](/circuit-design/) before powering anything new.
- **Moving to embedded Linux.** Start with the [Single Board Computers overview](/single-board-computers/) and the [Muse Pro Pi introduction](/single-board-computers/muse-pro-pi/).

## How this wiki works

**Verification status is explicit.** Every tutorial states whether it is a draft or
has been reviewed. A draft is written from cited sources, but it has not been
bench-tested for this wiki. Treat its wiring, code, and output as a starting
point, not a guarantee.

**Example values are labelled.** Pin numbers, addresses, and component values are
examples unless a page says otherwise. Board revisions and module pinouts differ,
so check the silkscreen and the datasheet for the part in your hand.

**Sources are cited.** Vendor specifications are presented as published, with a
link to the source. If a value has not been independently measured, the page says
so.

:::note[Safety first]
The wiki focuses on low-voltage DC electronics. Mains wiring, high-current
batteries, and anything that can injure you are out of scope. Disconnect power
before changing a circuit, and use a certified supply for anything that connects
to the wall.
:::
