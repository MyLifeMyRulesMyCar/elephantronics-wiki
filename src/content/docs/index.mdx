## How this wiki works

<div class="principle-grid">
  <div class="principle">
    <h3>Draft first</h3>
    <p>
      Every tutorial states whether it is a draft or has been reviewed. A draft is
      written from cited sources but has not been bench-tested for this wiki.
    </p>
  </div>
  <div class="principle">
    <h3>Examples labelled</h3>
    <p>
      Pin numbers, addresses, and component values are examples unless a page says
      otherwise. Board revisions and module pinouts differ.
    </p>
  </div>
  <div class="principle">
    <h3>Sources cited</h3>
    <p>
      Vendor specifications are presented as published, with a link. Values that
      have not been measured are not presented as verified.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
